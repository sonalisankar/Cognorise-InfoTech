# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics


The interactive Power BI dashboard provides insights into
e-commerce sales performance, revenue trends, and customer behavior.

E-Commerce_Sales_Customer_Analytics/Ecommerce-dashboard.png.pbix

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales performance and customer purchasing behavior using SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI. The goal is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

Through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL queries, and interactive dashboard development, this project examines revenue trends, product performance, customer purchasing patterns, and repeat customer behavior.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales performance and revenue trends.
- Identify top-performing products based on revenue.
- Analyze monthly revenue patterns and sales performance.
- Identify high-value customers and purchasing behavior.
- Measure repeat customer activity.
- Develop actionable insights to support business growth and customer retention.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation & Validation
- Reviewed and validated customer, order, product, and order-item datasets.
- Cleaned and prepared transactional data for analysis.
- Checked data consistency and accuracy.
- Created analysis-ready datasets.

### 2. SQL Analysis
Developed SQL queries to investigate key business performance indicators, including:

- Data validation and quality checks.
- Revenue by product category.
- Monthly revenue trends.
- Top 10 products by revenue.
- Top 10 customers by revenue.
- Repeat customer rate.

### 3. Python Analysis
Used Python and Jupyter Notebook to explore, clean, and prepare datasets for further analysis and visualization.

### 4. Power BI Dashboard
Developed an interactive dashboard to communicate sales performance and customer analytics.

The dashboard supports the analysis of revenue trends, product performance, and customer purchasing patterns.

## Key Business Questions

1. How does revenue vary over time?
2. Which products generate the highest revenue?
3. Which customers contribute the most to overall sales?
4. How does purchasing behavior differ between new and returning customers?
5. What opportunities exist to improve customer retention and sales performance?
