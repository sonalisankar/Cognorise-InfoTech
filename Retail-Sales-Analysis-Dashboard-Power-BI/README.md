# Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to identify trends, evaluate business performance, and generate actionable insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard. The analysis focuses on sales performance, product trends, and customer purchasing behavior to support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Objectives
- Evaluate overall retail sales performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Examine customer purchasing patterns.
- Identify opportunities to improve revenue and business performance.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- **DAX:** Measures and calculations for key performance indicators.
- **Power Query:** Data preparation and transformation.
- **CSV / Excel:** Dataset management and analysis.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation
Imported and prepared the retail sales dataset for analysis.

### 2. Data Analysis
Developed measures to evaluate sales performance, product trends, and key business metrics.

### 3. Dashboard Development
Created an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate findings through visualizations and key performance indicators.

## Key Business Questions
- What is the overall sales performance?
- Which products and categories generate the highest revenue?
- How do sales change over time?
- What patterns can be identified in customer purchasing behavior?
- Where are the greatest opportunities for improvement?
